The first thirty seconds decide more than the last thirty slides.

## The short version

Watching feels like progress. You finish a lesson, you feel informed, and you move on. But nothing you made exists yet, so nothing is holding you to the course.

## What to do instead

End every lesson with something you build, and have a real person look at it. That is the whole reason our batches are capped at 18.

- Build something small after every lesson
- Share it with someone who will be honest
- Fix one thing before moving on

> Good feedback is specific, kind and small enough to act on in an afternoon.
